## Minga — Demo script (≈ 2:30)

**[0:00 – 0:15] Pantalla de inicio de Minga**

> This is Minga. It helps a small shop buy from a supplier without either of them taking the
> risk first.
> Everything you'll see runs on the Stellar test network, with test money.
> The app is in Spanish, because it's built for shops in Argentina.

**[0:15 – 0:30] Mostrar la billetera conectada. Si está vacía, tocar «Cargar plata de prueba»**

> First, the shop owner connects her wallet.
> If the wallet is new and empty, Minga says so, and loads test money with one tap.
> We added this button after a real user got stuck at exactly this step.

**[0:30 – 1:00] Pestaña «Soy del comercio»: completar el pedido (monto 10, qué compra, 3 días) y repasar**

> Now Rosa, our shop owner, places an order.
> She pastes her supplier's code, writes the amount, what she's buying, and how many days she
> needs to check the goods when they arrive. Here, three days.
> Before paying, Minga shows her a summary, in plain words: how much, to whom, and how many
> days she has to check the goods.

**[1:00 – 1:20] Confirmar y firmar en la billetera. Esperar el cartel «Tu plata quedó guardada»**

> She signs with her wallet.
> And this is the key moment: the money leaves her wallet, but it doesn't go to the supplier
> yet. It's locked in a contract. Nobody can touch it. Not Rosa, not the supplier, not us.
> Rosa gets an order number to send to her supplier.

**[1:20 – 1:40] Pestaña «Soy proveedor»: buscar ese número de pedido**

> Now let's see it from the supplier's side.
> He types the order number and sees that the payment is guaranteed.
> So he can send the goods with confidence, even if he has never met Rosa.

**[1:40 – 2:00] Volver a «Soy del comercio»: confirmar que la mercadería llegó. Mostrar «Pago liberado»**

> When the goods arrive, Rosa confirms.
> Only then does the contract send the money to the supplier. Payment released.
> And if the order never arrives, Rosa can cancel and get all her money back.

**[2:00 – 2:15] Tocar «Ver el comprobante en la red» (se abre stellar.expert)**

> Every step is recorded on the Stellar network.
> Anyone can check it. Rosa and her supplier don't have to rely on each other's word.

**[2:15 – 2:30] Volver a la pantalla de Minga, o mostrar el logo**

> Today, small shops in Argentina trade on personal trust. It works, until it breaks.
> Minga puts the guarantee in the payment, not in the people.
> Many hands lift what one alone cannot.

## Consejos para grabar

- **Grabá primero la pantalla y después la voz.** Leer mientras hacés clics es difícil. Por
  separado, podés repetir la voz las veces que haga falta.
- **Firmar en la red tarda unos segundos.** Si queda un silencio largo, cortalo al editar.
- **Hablá despacio.** Es mejor que sobre tiempo a que falte.
